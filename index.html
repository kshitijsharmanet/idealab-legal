Idea Lab Publisher – Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Idea Lab Publisher is a personal tool used only by the owner of The Idea Lab (@theidealabstudio on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram) to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Terms of Service

  • The app may only be used by its owner, with accounts the owner controls.
  • It uploads videos the owner created and has the rights to.
  • It is provided as-is, with no warranty.

Privacy Policy

  • Data used: access tokens issued by TikTok, YouTube and Instagram when the owner signs in, and basic account info (account ID and username).
  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and used solely to upload the owner's videos. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent anywhere other than the platform the video is posted to.
  • No data is collected from any other person.
  • Revoking access in the platform's settings, or deleting the local token file, removes all stored data.
